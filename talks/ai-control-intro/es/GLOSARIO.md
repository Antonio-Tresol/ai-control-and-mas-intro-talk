## Numbers and dates

- Decimals with a point: 94.9%, 0.4%. Percent without a space: 26%.
- Four-digit numbers without a separator (1200); five or more with a thin space (70 000). Keep `~` and `>` as in the English.
- Dates: "8 jul 2026", "8 de julio de 2026" in prose; "13 h" for hours.

## Quotes

Quotes from English sources are translated and marked: add "(trad.)" after the source in the footer citation, e.g. "OpenAI 2026, p.4 (trad.)". Keep their meaning exact; never strengthen a claim.

## Fitting

Spanish runs about 20% longer. Keep the layout, positions and sizes of the English slide. If a text no longer fits (overflows its box, a headline goes past two lines at 72 px, a nowrap tag runs past its border), first choose a shorter wording, then widen the box only where the layout leaves room. Never go below 40 px (28 px footer only).
