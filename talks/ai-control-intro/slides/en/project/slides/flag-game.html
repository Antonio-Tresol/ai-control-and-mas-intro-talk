Flag Game (Pavlova & Tanaka 2026, arXiv 2609.19124): agents see private crops of a hidden flag; small swarms collapse onto a false belief, large ones polarize (France vs Peru at N = 64). Patching one in eight agents: mean gain 40% at N = 8, about 17% at N = 128. Of 533 agents active on the board, over 90% quickly joined the attack (METR 2026). In the paper the hidden flag is France (blue, white, red) and the wrong belief is Peru (red, white, red): no crop of Peru contains blue, so red and white crops pull toward Peru. At N = 4 the one agent whose crop shows blue is talked out of it.
