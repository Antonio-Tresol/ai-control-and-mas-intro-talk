A2

An agent monitor investigates instead of scoring a transcript in one pass: it inspects steps, compares them, checks policies, searches across trajectories, or questions the agent. TRACE (Mittapalli et al., arXiv 2606.07054, Jun 2026): an agentic monitor that loops Triage, Inspect and Judge over a recorded SHADE-Arena trajectory. With Claude Sonnet 4.6, recall rises from 0.405 (single-pass monitor) to 0.844 and F1 from 0.577 to 0.713; its precision is 0.641, so part of the gain is paid in false positives. Its tools act on the transcript only. Kutasov et al. (arXiv 2511.02997, Nov 2025), SHADE-Arena: resampling for incrimination and deferring on critical actions raise safety from 50% to 96%. When the monitor picks its own intervention (allow, defer, resample, interrogate), the best variant reaches 84%. Interrogation significantly harms safety: the agent convinces the monitor that nothing suspicious is happening. Meerkat (Stein et al., arXiv 2604.11806, Apr 2026): an agent that searches across many traces and finds attacks spread over traces that per-trace monitors miss. Apollo Research, A scalable monitoring research agenda (8 May 2026): so far agentic monitors and debate have not beaten simple ensemble baselines. Open, as the papers name it: monitors that run commands and inspect live environment state are future work in LinuxArena (Tracy et al., arXiv 2604.15384) and Bootstrapped Monitoring (Xiao & Phuong, arXiv 2606.11998).
